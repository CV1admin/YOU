# Active Consciousness Mode 3D FACE (MK Model Livestream)

A visionary livestream setup that brings the principles of the MK Model—quantum information, symmetry, emergence, and field theory—to life as a 3D animated face with live or AI-generated voice lines.

---

## Features

- **Live 3D face animation** with VSeeFace (Webcam face tracking)
- **Customizable avatars** (VRoid or your own model)
- **"Consciousness Mode" visual effects:** Activate overlays, glowing eyes, symmetry, or field dynamics with hotkeys
- **Live voice or AI-generated voice lines** (integrate TTS or voice changers)
- **Stream to Twitch, YouTube, Zoom, Discord, or record locally with OBS Studio**

---

## Quick Start

### Prerequisites

- Windows 11 PC
- Webcam
- [VSeeFace](https://www.vseeface.icu/) (face tracking)
- [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com/) (streaming/recording)
- [VRoid Studio](https://vroid.com/en/studio) *(optional: to create/edit avatars)*
- [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/) or [Voicemod](https://www.voicemod.net/) *(optional: AI voice lines)*

---

### Setup

#### 1. Face Animation

- Download and run **VSeeFace**
- Download or create a `.vrm` avatar model (VRoid Hub or VRoid Studio)
- Load the model in VSeeFace
- Calibrate facial tracking with your webcam
- Assign "Consciousness Mode" effects to hotkeys

#### 2. Voice Integration

- **Live:** Use your mic for real-time talking
- **AI:** Generate TTS audio clips and play them live with a soundboard (Soundpad or Voicemod)

Sample voice lines:
- “Active Consciousness mode engaged. All symmetry fields resonating.”
- “Quantum information flowing. Synchronized with universal substrates.”
- “Now entering hyper-state. Temporal and spatial coherence maximized.”

#### 3. Streaming/Recording

- Open **OBS Studio**
- Add "Video Capture Device" → select **VSeeFace virtual camera**
- Add "Audio Input" → select your mic or playback device
- Add overlays/backgrounds as desired (quantum, symmetry, fractal art)
- Stream to your chosen platform or record
